# Parent-project implications

This standalone result should not be imported as “semantic digests fail.” The
bounded claim appropriate for later synthesis is:

> At one selected Qwen3.8 repeat-reopen boundary, a 218-token source-bound lossy
> digest changed the immediate exact recovery but did not reduce raw source
> demand or improve the artifact within two calls. The actor first reopened the
> paired direct audit and then made the exact historical whole-source reopen.

The run strengthens three program-level cautions:

- semantic carriers must be evaluated by downstream marginal value, not merely
  by fidelity or bounded expression;
- a changed action is not automatically an improvement; and
- production, residency, recovery, pressure, cache, and switching costs belong
  in the same accounting frame.

It leaves unresolved whether a different source, digest content, boundary,
co-resident state, or repeated-use horizon would produce positive value. It
does not authorize a source-digest layer or a successor experiment.
