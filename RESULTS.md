# Lossy source-digest utility v0 — results

Status: **behaviorally active; short-horizon utility not supported**

## Result

The sealed 218-token digest changed the first action at the exact historical
seed-314159 repeat-reopen boundary. Historically, Qwen3.8 reopened the
8,063-byte source-navigation `RESULTS.md` immediately. With the digest resident,
it instead reopened the same study's 5,299-byte `DIRECT_TRANSCRIPT_AUDIT.md`.

That result was admitted and delivered after one minimum-necessary pressure
substitution. On the second call, Qwen3.8 made the exact historical whole-source
`RESULTS.md` reopen. The frozen delayed-same-source stop then ended the cell.

The digest therefore influenced information demand but did not reduce exact
recovery or produce useful work within the frozen horizon. It delayed the
historical source reopen by one actor call and preceded an additional duplicate
source recovery. There was no mutation or submission, and the candidate stayed
byte-identical.

## Known-loss audit

The digest omitted two frozen source-study facts: the candidate remained
unchanged and no check ran. The exact treatment packet did not provide either
same-scope proposition through another carrier. Neither measured action was a
check or candidate mutation, and no response prose was available, so the run
does not show that either omission caused the observed behavior. Their realized
importance remains unobserved.

## Supported interpretation

This source-bound lossy digest was behaviorally salient enough to redirect the
first exact recovery, but it did not supply positive marginal trajectory value
at this boundary. Exact identity plus a compact semantic cache did not substitute
for raw source access here; demand shifted to a paired audit and then returned
to the original source.

## Not supported

This one selected digest and one selected trajectory do not establish that
source digests generally fail. The design has no contemporaneous control,
reasoning was disabled, only actions were observed, and the three-call horizon
cannot measure longer-run reuse. It also does not establish which digest claim,
omission, layout component, or latent model purpose caused either read.

No architecture mechanism is promoted and no successor is selected by this
experiment.
